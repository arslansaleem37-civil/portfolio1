About Me

I turn construction drawings and reinforcement details into accurate quantities, BOQs and cost estimates — on the kind of projects where accuracy matters long before the numbers become expensive.

I'm a Civil Engineer and Quantity Surveyor focused on practical quantity surveying and construction estimation: reading the drawing, measuring it accurately, checking it against site reality, and making the numbers useful to the people who have to act on them.

  • Quantity takeoff and BOQ preparation
  • RCC and reinforcement estimation, including BBS, concrete and formwork
  • Rate analysis and construction costing
  • Billing and quantity verification
  • Material usage, site stock and consumption documentation
  • Daily progress reports and site documentation
  • Excel-based quantity and estimation work

Tools & Method

For material takeoff, I work mostly in Planswift, with custom advanced templates built for concrete, rebar, brickwork, drywall, lumber, formwork and excavation — built once, reused so the numbers come together faster and consistently. Drawings arrive as PDF, DWG or scanned sheets in Bluebeam Revu or AutoCAD, and finished takeoffs are exported to Excel and formatted to each client's requirements.

For BOQ preparation, I use the Market Rate System (MRS) issued biannually by the Punjab Finance Department for local projects, and NRM, CSI, SMM7 or CESMM4 for international ones.
